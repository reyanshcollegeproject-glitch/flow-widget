The 5 Core Layers of Flow Widget

01

Non-Activating Floating HUD

Uses macOS NSPanel with .nonactivatingPanel. It hovers over any app without stealing keyboard focus from Slack, Notion, or your IDE.

02

Microphone Stream & RMS

Captures 16kHz mono audio via AVAudioEngine. Computes live Root Mean Square (RMS) energy to power the dynamic waveform visualizer.

03

Fast Speech-to-Text

Feeds audio frames to Apple's native SFSpeechRecognizer (offline/free) or cloud Whisper/Deepgram for instant transcription.

04

AI Polish & Filter

Runs the proprietary prompt logic: removes fillers (um, uh, like), resolves human self-corrections ("2 wait 3"), and formats punctuation.

05

Universal Direct Injection

Copies the polished result to NSPasteboard and simulates a Cmd + V keypress via CGEvent directly into your active cursor position.
