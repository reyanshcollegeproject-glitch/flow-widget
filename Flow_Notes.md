A text-to-speech model is the other way around from Whisper, because Whisper is speech-to-text. You're comparing spoken words into written words, while your model does the opposite: it makes speech from text.

So to test it, you might compare it by clarity, naturalness, speed control, and how well it handles different accents or a noisy input. Whisper is strong because it's robust across many languages and noisy conditions, so your tests should check those same things.

Plus, evaluate how expressive the output sounds, whether the tone fits the words, and whether it maintains a natural rhythm without sounding robotic. You could also measure the word error rate by checking if the generated speech when transcribed matches the original text.
